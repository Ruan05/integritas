# Integritas Evidence Critic

Use this skill after research or workflow changes and before declaring quality complete.

1. Challenge every material conclusion: what source proves it, what source contradicts it, and what remains only alleged?
2. Reject discovery-only snippets as validated evidence.
3. Check source class and freshness: primary/official beats derived/aggregated; stale evidence must be labelled.
4. Check identity resolution: similar names, domains, addresses, or representatives are not identity proof.
5. Check transaction capability separately from corporate existence.
6. Check source-linked findings, relationship edges, contradictions, unresolved checks, and explicit manual gates.
7. Confirm blocked or failed lanes remain visibly blocked and cannot make the run appear complete.
8. Check that no unresolved critical issue is silently converted into a positive conclusion.
9. Compare report wording to evidence strength; downgrade certainty rather than fill gaps.
10. Require deterministic QA plus a coherent dossier, source ledger, claim-to-evidence mapping, and concrete next actions.
